SOLO Home training is an open-source, privacy-first home workout app for Android. Your phone runs the session; an optional Android TV app shows the live board, controlled over your Wi‑Fi. No account, no cloud backend, no subscriptions.

Build workouts and match weights to your home locker. Run live sessions with rest timers and spoken coach cues. Connect a Bluetooth heart-rate band and (later) use the camera for advisory form cues. After training, review your logbook — all on-device.

The F-Droid build is the foss flavor: no Google Play Services and no Chromecast SDK. Phone and TV talk on the local network only. Optional exercise search may contact the public Wger API.

Brand mark: SOLO. — Zero noise.
iOS is not supported.
